The BMW X7 is a luxury SUV that combines spaciousness with high-end features. It offers a range of powerful engines, including a 3.0-liter BMW TwinPower Turbo inline 6-cylinder and a 4.4-liter BMW M TwinPower Turbo V8. The X7 is equipped with advanced technology, including the BMW iDrive 7.0 system, a large touchscreen display, and a premium sound system. Safety features include active driving assistance and a comprehensive airbag system.

The BMW i7 features a 101.7 kWh battery pack, a range of up to 625 km (WLTP), and can charge from 10-80% in approximately 34 minutes with a DC fast charger. Performance varies by model, with the i7 xDrive60 offering \(536\) horsepower and a \(0-100\) km/h time of \(4.7\) seconds, while the M70 xDrive model produces up to \(657\) horsepower. The sedan has a length of \(5,391\) mm, a width of \(1,950\) mm, and a wheelbase of \(3,215\) mm.

The BMW M4 is a high-performance sports coupe that offers thrilling driving dynamics and a powerful engine lineup. It features a 3.0-liter BMW M TwinPower Turbo inline 6-cylinder engine, delivering impressive acceleration and handling. The M4 is equipped with advanced technology, including the BMW iDrive system, a digital instrument cluster, and a range of driver assistance features. Its aggressive design and aerodynamic enhancements make it a standout on the road.
